## Governing rule: the House notices
Mechanics are not illustrations of prose. A mechanic occurs only when the House notices something.

Most text receives no response. Stillness is the default state of the House.

A semantic correspondence is not authorization. A sentence containing love does not automatically receive LOVE; a sentence containing change does not automatically receive CHANGE. A mechanic must justify interruption, not merely correspondence.

If static typography carries the moment, leave it alone.

## Production vocabulary is opt-in
Presence in the mechanics library does **not** authorize use in the live book.

The shared mechanics runtime is now an archive/workshop shelf. Archived mechanics remain preserved and callable for labs and future auditioning, but production choreography must not invoke them unless Josh explicitly promotes that mechanic for a narrative requirement.
