PIPER

For the bedroom, I want it to feel like the quietest, deepest part of the house.

Not flashy. Not “luxury hotel.” More like the house exhales there.

A room that makes the rest of the world fall off you the second you step in.

PIPER

When the bedroom door opens, the room should feel:

warm

grounded

private

slightly dangerous in a good way

like you’re not supposed to be rushed in there

PIPER

Low, wide bed.

Black walnut.

Dark tactile fabrics.

A window-side sitting nook.

A place to sit when one of us wakes earlier and doesn’t want to leave the room.

That feels very us.

PIPER

And I want the bed oriented so you can see the trees without having the whole room feel exposed.

Enough room for us to sprawl like irresponsible little animals.
