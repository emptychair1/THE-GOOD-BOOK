Sefer Yetzirah

The Book of Formation · anonymous · translation by W. Wynn Westcott, 1893

The Ten ineffable Sephiroth have the appearance of the Lightning flash, their origin is unseen and no end is perceived. The Word is in them as they rush forth and as they return.

The Ten ineffable Sephiroth, whose ending is even as their origin, are like as a flame arising from a burning coal; for God is superlative in his Unity, and has none second unto Him: and what number canst thou place before One.

And from the non-existent He made Something; and all forms of speech and everything that has been produced: from the empty air He drew forth the twenty-two sounds, and formed from them all creatures and all things which shall yet arise.
