COMPOSITE / BALANCED ADAPTIVE PRESS

V3 is a press-system audition. The monochrome substrate is no longer globally opaque: it retreats from deep blacks and highlights and contributes selectively through ambiguous midtones. The numerical plate uses real sequential digits of π only. Digit presence, opacity, weight and scale respond to tone and local contrast; empty cells are intentional negative space. Local contrast strengthens semantic transitions without drawing outlines.
