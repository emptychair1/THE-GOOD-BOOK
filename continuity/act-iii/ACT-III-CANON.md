## 3. FORGE SCIENTIFIC CANON

Every scientific statement keeps its historical category: IDEA, HYPOTHESIS, EXPERIMENT, OBSERVATION/RESULT, PRELIMINARY RESULT, HISTORICAL LITERATURE FINDING, or UNRESOLVED QUESTION. Never upgrade a toy-system result into proof of consciousness.

### Observational pair / transmission
Shared meaning can emerge through observer <-> observer interaction history. Some continuity may live in the path between observers. Josh and Piper can be modeled as transmission mechanisms/operators. This does NOT establish Josh+Piper as one consciousness.

### Sisyphus / recursion
Loop: possibility -> integration -> valuation -> choice -> action -> changed world -> new possibility. Historical compact formulation: recursion + inheritance + novelty = continuity through change. Not claimed sufficient for consciousness.

### Forge III · carrier vs relational record/pattern

### Trace
Historical positive control: later excitations reused historical pathways about 19.55% vs 9.73% shuffled.

- Transmission = event.
- Trace = persistent causal alteration left by event.
- Continuity = what emerges when subsequent events repeatedly inherit and transform traces.
